# Patient photos, treatment progress and follow-up

Apply `supabase/migrations/20261025_add_patient_progress_and_treatment_review.sql` in the Supabase SQL editor after the existing migrations, including `20261024_add_online_consultations_and_prescriptions.sql`. This migration creates the private photo bucket, patient photo/progress tables, procedure dates, and atomic treatment save function. It preserves existing treatment records and moves completed records to the Finalize review step. The migration has been tested locally; this repository change does not apply it to the hosted database.

In the doctor portal, Consultation > Patient record has patient profile photo upload/replacement. Before/after and progress photos are available exclusively under Treatment Workspace > Patient record. A patient's profile photo is shared between the two workspaces. Before/after photos cannot be uploaded against a consultation appointment, including through the API.

Treatment patient records support dated before/after/progress photos, area filters, side-by-side comparison, captions, procedure/progress notes, and requested follow-up consultation dates. Procedure notes and requested follow-up dates from saved treatment charts also appear in the patient's timeline. The timeline remains available in consultation records. Requested dates are recommendations; they do not reserve appointment slots. Linked booked consultation follow-ups are displayed separately from recommended dates.

Treatment charting follows Consent > Anesthesia > Procedure > Post-care > Finalize. Advancing validates and saves earlier stages. Procedure descriptions, performed dates and follow-up instructions are retained. Finalizing completes the chart and appointment in one database transaction. Completed charts are available for review; later treatment photos and progress notes can still be added through Patient record.

Photos are available only to doctors/superadmins through authenticated server routes. Storage is private; the browser receives expiring signed URLs rather than public object URLs. Uploads are decoded, resized to 1600 pixels, re-encoded as WebP, and stripped of source metadata. The upload accepts JPG, PNG and WebP; the browser resizes files before sending them. Each processed image is limited to 3 MB. Profile replacement removes the old object after its new record is saved.

Validation: `npm test` covers calendar/clinical validation, role access, private storage, image decoding, cleanup, consultation photo restrictions, and atomic/read-only treatment completion. `npx playwright test tests/e2e/consultation.spec.cjs` uses mock API data to check charting, uploads, comparison, follow-up dates and mobile layout; it does not exercise the hosted database or real patient records.
